# Open-content offer: free course, paid consultations

**Status:** accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-24
**Amended:** 2026-10-24 — renamed from open-core; same day: paid beta, seats, certificate, and the 299 BYN library SKU dropped; the paid offering is reduced to 1:1 consultations.

Replace the README's "100% free" / `$5` per-week model with an open-content offer:

- **Free:** the entire 8-week course content — the public repository plus a guided Telegram subscription feed.
- **Paid:** single ad-hoc 1:1 consultations under НПД (ADR-0004).

Pricing: 60 BYN per 60-minute session for the first 10 sessions, then 90 BYN. There is no cohort, no seats, no certificate, no library SKU, and no course platforms. A future paid product (cohort, library, certificate) can return only through an ADR-0004 revisit, which requires ИП.

## Why

Per-week micro-purchases underprice support and fragment the learning path. A cohort demanded platform logistics, refund policy, and ИП cost before anything was validated. A free subscriber-based course builds audience with zero infrastructure, and consultations validate paid demand immediately.

## Consequences

- Free content must be genuinely useful, not a teaser.
- Paid value is direct access and advice; never gate the content.
- Do not sell individual weeks, packages, or subscriptions.
- Revisit pricing after ~15 sessions; a support or capstone-review package may be revisited then.
