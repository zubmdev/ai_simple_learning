# Dual-brand: Vibe Coding entry, Agentic Engineering paid product

**Status:** accepted

**Date:** 2026-10-24

**Amended:** 2026-10-24 — the paid offering narrowed to 1:1 consultations; the cohort was dropped (ADR-0002).

We keep **Vibe Coding Course** as the public umbrella because it carries existing recognition and search demand, but sell paid services under the **Agentic Engineering** banner (RU subtitle: **AI-инженерия** — 1:1 consultations) because "vibe coding" signals low rigor and is past peak. The free entry remains **Vibe Coding Day 0** and leads to the paid category.

## Considered options

- **Full rename now:** cleaner premium positioning, but loses the existing audience and search traffic before the paid offer is validated.
- **Separate brands:** maximum separation, but doubles the marketing and support surface.
- **Dual-brand with a migration path:** keep the umbrella, sell the paid category, and revisit a full rename after 100 paid learners.

## Consequences

- Two names must be used consistently: the free entry uses "Vibe Coding"; the paid offer uses "Agentic Engineering".
- Every free lesson needs one clear CTA to the Telegram subscription or consultation booking.
- Revisit the umbrella name after the launch, not during it.
