# Free course, НПД-only consultations; ИП only when scaling

**Status:** accepted

**Date:** 2026-10-24

**Amended:** 2026-10-24 — paid cohort dropped; revenue is consultations under НПД; ИП deferred with explicit triggers.

НПД cannot cover the course. The МНС letter of 20.01.2023 № 3-1-13/00223 explicitly treats programming courses, including online courses, as educational activity and says only organizations and individual entrepreneurs may provide them. НПД applies to tutoring/consulting, not to educational programs.

Source: https://www.nalog.gov.by/clarifications/clarifications/16195/

## Decision

1. The course is published **free** (repo + Telegram subscription). Nothing of the course is sold.
2. **Paid revenue:** single ad-hoc 1:1 consultations under НПД — 60 BYN intro for the first 10 sessions, then 90 BYN; 2-3 slots/week; a чек per session; advisory framing only. Consultation refunds: full refund on cancellation ≥24 hours before the session; no refunds after the session.
3. ИП is **not required** while revenue is consultations only. Reassess when (a) the НПД annual limit of 60,000 BYN approaches, or (b) any course/cohort/certificate/library product returns — then register ИП (ОКЭД 85.59) before accepting a single course payment.
4. No paid course platforms are in scope (Boosty excluded; PaidSub.by/Lava.top/GetCourse dropped with the cohort). Consultation payment: transfer to a personal account before the session.
5. This ADR is a product decision, not legal advice; optional written accountant confirmation.

## Course vs consultation boundary

Validated against the source letter. The test is what money is paid for: an organized learning process on the provider's program is educational activity (organizations and ИП only, online included). Tutoring stays purely advisory, and programming is explicitly named in the letter as "обучение", not tutoring.

| НПД consultation (allowed) | Educational activity (needs ИП) |
|---|---|
| One-off session on the learner's own problem or project | Sessions run on the provider's fixed program and schedule |
| Each session is booked separately | Sold as "lesson N of M" or a package following a program |
| Output: analysis and recommendations for the learner's own task | Output: course progress, homework, review, certificate |
| Marketed as разбор / консультация | Marketed as урок, занятие, обучение |

## Boundary rules

- Publishing course content for free is legal marketing; nothing is implemented for pay.
- Paid consultations are single sessions with a fixed per-session price; no packages structured as a program ("8 консультаций = курс").
- Do not tether paid sessions to course weeks ("buy the review of Week N"): free course plus paid weekly sessions together form a paid educational program.
- НПД mechanics: registration in the «Профессиональный доход» app, a чек for every service, and the annual income limit of 60,000 BYN.

## Why

A cohort demanded platform logistics, refund policy, and ИП cost before any validation. A free course carries all the marketing; consultations validate paid demand immediately, with the МНС letter's only constraint being to stay advisory. The consultation-only model keeps the legal surface minimal: НПД covers it, and no educational program is sold.

## Consequences

- The plan has no beta, no seats, no course refund policy, and no platform fees.
- Consult revenue before ИП validates paid demand; the 60,000 BYN/year НПД limit is the revenue ceiling, and approaching it triggers the ИП assessment.
- Repeat clients must bring a new problem each time; standing weekly slots = a program = refuse.
- No educational license is required either way: the same letter confirms обучающие курсы need no Ministry of Education license — only registration as an org/ИП and standard consumer-protection compliance.
- If a paid course product ever returns, the original constraint applies: only organizations and ИП may implement educational programs.
